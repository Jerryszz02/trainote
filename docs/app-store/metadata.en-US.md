## Subtitle

Track workouts & meals offline

## Promotional Text

Keep workouts, meals and body records together. Connect Apple Health if you choose, and generate an on-device AI report to organize facts and available suggestions.

## Description

Trainote helps you log every set and meal and review your everyday fitness records. The interface supports English and Simplified Chinese. Basic features work offline without an account.

WORKOUTS
• Log weight and reps, bodyweight exercises, holds and cardio
• Review previous performance and use rest timers
• Create and reuse templates, edit history or repeat a workout
• Browse a bilingual exercise library

MEALS
• Record breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
• Save favorite foods and meals, or copy a meal
• Adjust portions or amounts per 100 grams and review nutrients
• Set daily calorie and macronutrient targets

REVIEWS AND REFERENCE
• Review weekly activity, personal records, weight trends and target history
• See muscle readiness estimates based on records, with unknown results when evidence is missing
• Read rule-based reports or choose to generate an on-device AI report
• The bundled model selects existing facts and allowed suggestions without sending records to an AI provider
• Reading a report does not change a target or start a workout; you confirm whether to adopt a suggestion

YOUR CHOICES
• Read the privacy and usage notice on first launch and choose whether to continue
• Apple Health is a separate, optional, read-only connection; manual recording works without it
• Turn off on-device AI, withdraw notice acknowledgment, manage Health access or delete report history in Settings
• Export and restore local JSON backups
• No advertising, tracking or telemetry

Records are stored on your device. System backups and files you choose to export are managed by iOS settings and your chosen storage service. The model is bundled with the app; generating a report does not require a model download. If resources are insufficient or generation fails, a rule-based report is shown.

Trainote provides recording tools and everyday fitness information for healthy adults, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Nutrition and recovery suggestions should not be assumed suitable for children or people with special health needs. Recovery indicators are not a percentage recovered or an injury probability. AI may miss relevant details; check the original records.
